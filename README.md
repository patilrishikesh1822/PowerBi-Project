
Play Store Data Analysis - Power BI Dashboard

📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project provides a comprehensive analysis of the Google Play Store dataset. The interactive dashboard is designed to uncover insights regarding app categories, user installations, pricing, and user ratings. It helps developers, marketers, and business analysts understand market trends, user preferences, and the competitive landscape of the Android app market.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

At a glance, the dashboard tracks the following overall metrics:

Total Installs: 168 Billion

Total Reviews: 5 Billion

Total Apps: 9,639

Average Reviews per App: 444.15K

📑 Dashboard Pages & Features

The report is structured into four main analytical views:

1. Data Overview

Count of App by Category: Visualizes the distribution of apps across various categories using Bar and Area charts.

Top Categories by Volume: Family (1,972 apps), Game (1,144 apps), and Tools (843 apps) dominate the Play Store in terms of sheer volume.

2. Rating & Review Trends

Avg Rating and Total Reviews by App and Rating: A scatter plot analysis showing the correlation between high ratings (4.0 - 4.7) and total review volumes.

Total Reviews by Category (Treemap): Highlights the most engaged categories, led by Game, Communication, Social, and Family.

3. Top Categories

Top 10 Categories By Installs: Column chart revealing that Game (35.1bn) and Communication (32.6bn) are the undisputed leaders in user acquisition, followed by Productivity (14.2bn) and Social (11.5bn).

Total App Cost by Category (Treemap): Identifies where paid apps generate the most value/cost, with Finance, Family, Lifestyle, and Medical leading the pack.

4. User Feedback

Installs by App Top 15 (Pie Chart): Highlights powerhouse applications driving the install numbers, including Subway Surfers, Google Drive, Google News, Google Photos, Instagram, Candy Crush, and WhatsApp.

Number of Apps by Rating: A detailed breakdown of app ratings, showing a strong concentration of apps rated between 4.0 and 4.7.

💡 Key Insights

Gaming is King: The Game category is the most lucrative and popular, leading in both total installations (35.1 Billion) and total user reviews.

Communication is Essential: Despite having fewer apps overall compared to the Family category, Communication apps drive massive installation numbers (32.6 Billion), largely due to giants like WhatsApp, Messenger, and Skype.

Monetization happens in Niche Categories: The Finance and Medical categories show up strongly in the "Total App Cost" visual, indicating that users are more willing to pay for highly specialized, utility-driven, or financial applications.

Ratings Skew Positive: The vast majority of top-performing apps maintain an average rating above 4.0, indicating that high user satisfaction is a baseline requirement for high install volumes.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Business Intelligence Tool: Microsoft Power BI

Data Visualization: Custom DAX measures, Treemaps, Scatter Plots, Pie Charts, and Clustered Column/Line Charts.
